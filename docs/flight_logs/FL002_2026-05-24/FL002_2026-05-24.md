
# FL002 — 2026-05-24

## Flight Summary

- Three flights total; all successful takeoffs and soft landings.
- Flight 1: ~2 min. Takeoff, a couple circuits, soft landing.
- Flight 2: ~1–2 min. Takeoff, circuits, power-off gliding (several attempts), soft landing.
- Flight 3: Short. Launch, straight and level, soft landing.

## Observations

- Slight pitch oscillations (~1 Hz) persisted throughout all three flights, most noticeable
  in straight-and-level cruise at lower airspeeds. Roll remained stable.
- Power-off gliding behaved well; pitch authority felt adequate.

## Root Cause Analysis

1 Hz hunting in pitch during cruise is characteristic of an **attitude loop Kp that is too high**.
`att.pitch.kp = 200` was commanding aggressive rate corrections (up to 60 deg/s) for small pitch
errors. At lower airspeeds, reduced aerodynamic damping amplifies the overshoot, producing the
observed ~1 Hz cycle. The rate loop (unchanged since FL001) is not the cause — roll is stable.

## Tuning Actions

| Parameter | Previous | New | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| `att.pitch.kp` | 200.0 | **150.0** | Reduce attitude P gain ~25% to dampen pitch hunting |
| `att.pitch.outlimit` | 60.0 deg/s | **45.0 deg/s** | Cap max rate demand; most effective at low airspeeds |

Rate loop parameters (`rate.pitch.kp`, `rate.pitch.ti`, `rate.pitch.td`) left unchanged.

## Expected Outcome

- ~1 Hz pitch hunting should be eliminated in straight-and-level cruise.
- Pitch response may feel slightly softer; if sluggish, raise `att.pitch.kp` to 165 as a middle ground.
- If any residual hunting remains, reduce `att.pitch.outlimit` further to 40 deg/s before touching Kp again.
